# AISNQ

## Overview

AISNQ is a progressive web app for college students who want to cut down the time it takes to prepare a reviewer. A student uploads learning material (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, or a website URL). The system uses AI (a free Gemini provider) to generate shorter, better-organized study notes from it, and a practice quiz based on those notes.

The problem it addresses is the slow, labor-intensive process of manually preparing reviewers, which feels even heavier when a module has a large scope. This is supported by a survey of 105 respondents, where labor-intensive reviewer preparation was the most common complaint (25.7%). The system’s promise is to reduce the time it takes to prepare a reviewer.

Users: College students are the primary users in the prototype. Teacher and tutor accounts (for example, a teacher who creates a subject and approves students) may be added, but they are not yet confirmed and depend on upcoming professor interviews.

Limitations: The notes and quiz are AI-generated and may contain errors or omit details. The quiz measures the notes, not the entire module, and a disclaimer says so when a module is created. The system does not replace studying, so students are responsible for checking the output against the original material. Uploaded files are not stored. Only the filename or URL is shown as a source label.
